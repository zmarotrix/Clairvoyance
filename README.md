# Clairvoyance

A modern quest helper for WoW Forever, built on **NorgannonDB** and **NorgannonDB Core**. It's
part of the NorgannonDB suite, together with Oracle, Foresight and Archivum.

**v0.1 (in development):**
- **Objectives:** every unfinished objective on the world map, minimap and compass, in the
  quest's colour. Continent maps show one marker per objective.
- **Turn-ins:** the game's own "?", grey until the quest is complete.
- **The quest tracker:**
  - every quest in your log (hide any), sorted Nearest, Zone, Level or Log
  - live progress, and party members' progress (Questie-compatible)
  - distance and bearing to the nearest target, and the turn-in line
  - a "use item" button that works in combat
  - hover to highlight on the map, ctrl-click to point the arrow, right-click for actions
- **Map filter chips:** choose what's drawn.

Still to come (see the plan): available quests, tooltips, track anything, gathering and rares,
opt-in automation, settings and the first-run welcome.
